Forest art imported from the user-provided Chained Echoes Texture2D export.

Primary vegetation files are renamed copies of the original tree02/tree03,
tree04-tree06, bush01-bush04, and shadow5 PNGs. The 16x16 terrain
path tiles are deterministic crops/composites derived from Atlas_Fiorwoods_1.png.
Canopy and grass/ground modules are direct Sprite_Fiorwoods exports. They are
layered inside solid forest cells and across walkable terrain respectively.
